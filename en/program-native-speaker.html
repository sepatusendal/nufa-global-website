ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Native English Speaker

Our Native English Speaker Division manages the entire process of placing native English-speaking teachers at partner schools — from recruitment and training to school matching based on the school's character, through to work visa support.

Every placement is tracked through monthly performance evaluations accessible to the school via the School Portal, keeping teaching quality consistent throughout the assignment.
