ABOUT THE PROGRAM

Immersion Program Australia

Part of NUFA Global Education's Immersion Program, the Australia track runs for 7–14 days with our official partners — Albright Institute Australia and the Department for Education South Australia. Students attend classes in an Australian school environment, live with a local homestay family, and take part in cultural exchange.

The Australia track connects with other NUFA programs in the same country — Study Abroad Australia for students looking for a longer-term study path. See full partner details on our Global Network page.
