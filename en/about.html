About Us

An education ecosystem connecting schools with the world.

PT NUFA Global Education is a strategic partner for Indonesian schools in building an integrated global education ecosystem.

Company Overview

Not just an English course or education travel service.

PT NUFA Global Education is an international education company focused on improving education quality in Indonesia through English programs, international learning experiences, teacher development, and strategic collaboration with education institutions across countries.

We are a strategic partner for schools in building a global education ecosystem that prepares Indonesia's next generation to be competent, confident, and internationally competitive global citizens — integrating education, technology, international partnerships, and experiential learning into one connected ecosystem.

Vision

A leading international education company

To become Indonesia's leading international education company, connecting schools, educators, students, and global institutions through innovative, sustainable, and impactful education solutions.

Mission

Our 5 core commitments

  • Help Indonesian schools raise their education quality to international standards
  • Develop active, applicable English communication skills
  • Provide global learning experiences through international programs
  • Bridge collaboration between Indonesian education institutions and countries worldwide
  • Develop education technology that supports learning transformation
